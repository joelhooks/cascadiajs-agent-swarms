# Talk Prep Method

Source: https://www.deconstructconf.com/blog/how-to-prepare-a-talk

## Core principle

A talk is a verbal performance, not an outline, article, or slide deck. Preparation should mostly be talking out loud.

Do not silently polish notes and mistake that for rehearsal. That prepares you to silently polish notes, not to stand on stage and speak.

## Process to steal for CascadiaJS

1. Stand up and talk through the topic with no audience.
2. Project voice, walk around, behave like stage conditions are real.
3. Capture only short note slides when a useful phrase/idea appears.
4. Repeat once per day, not many times in one day.
5. Let partial forgetting improve the talk; do not memorize exact wording.
6. Watch for high-energy moments and make the talk move toward them.
7. Group note slides into 4–8 sections only after several spoken runs.
8. Keep slides rough until the spoken shape stabilizes.
9. Cut when the talk becomes annoyingly long.
10. Practice the final version enough that delivery survives stage stress.

## Constraints for CascadiaJS

- Target a dense 25-minute talk even if the slot allows longer.
- Avoid a Cloudflare product tour. Cloudflare is substrate evidence, not the story.
- Avoid repo-autopsy throat-clearing. Sources are there to sharpen the frame, not to be recited.
- Cut anything that does not move toward the playable swarm/work cartridge idea.
